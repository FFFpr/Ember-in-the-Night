# Street — locked 3D camera

## Goal

Exterior street / facade from a locked camera (eye-level or slight setback).

## Day → night (20 s)

| Time | Look |
|------|------|
| 0–10 s | Directional sun + bright sky/ambient |
| 10–20 s | Sun off or near-zero; moon/ambient blue; window and lamp omnis on |

Drive with an `AnimationPlayer` or scripted tween on light energies and environment background.
